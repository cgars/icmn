# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Be constructive, assume good faith, critique ideas rather than people, and respect privacy and confidentiality.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and sustained disruption. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent possible.

This compact policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
